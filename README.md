# Advance Repair

> **More ways to repair your gear. More choices. Same Minecraft progression.**

Ever damaged your favorite gear and wished you had more options to repair it?

**Advance Repair** expands Minecraft's anvil repair system by allowing additional materials to restore durability to damaged equipment.

The goal isn't to make repairs free or ridiculously powerful.

The goal is to give you **more choices while keeping Minecraft's progression and economy intact.**

---

## What Does Advance Repair Do?

Advance Repair adds alternative repair materials to the anvil.

Instead of being limited to the traditional repair material for an item, you can use other materials to restore a portion of its durability.

Different materials provide different repair amounts, so choosing what to use actually matters.

### Example Repair Values

| Equipment Tier | Repair Material | Durability Restored |
|---|---|---:|
| Netherite | Diamond | 80% |
| Netherite | Iron | 50% |
| Netherite | Gold | 40% |
| Netherite | Copper | 40% |
| Diamond | Iron | 60% |
| Diamond | Gold | 45% |
| Diamond | Copper | 40% |
| Iron | Gold | 70% |
| Iron | Copper | 40% |
| Leather | Leather | 100% |
| Chainmail | Chain | 60% |
| Chainmail | Iron | 50% |

> These values are designed around the idea of **useful alternatives rather than replacements for normal Minecraft progression.**

---

## Built With Economy in Mind

Advance Repair is designed to avoid making durability meaningless.

Repairs still require **XP**, and the cost can change depending on the item.

For example:

- Normal item → lower XP cost
- Enchanted item → higher XP cost
- Trimmed item → higher XP cost
- Enchanted + trimmed item → highest cost

This means powerful equipment still has a meaningful repair cost.

The purpose of the mod is to give you another option when you're low on the usual repair material — **not to remove the resource economy entirely.**

## Repair Information

Wondering what you can use to repair an item?

Advance Repair adds repair information directly to item tooltips.

You'll be able to see things such as:

**Repair with Diamond: 80%**

**Repair with Iron: 50%**

**XP Cost: 5–12 levels**

No need to memorize every recipe.

Just look at the item.

## Configurable Mod Support

Advance Repair isn't limited to vanilla Minecraft.

The mod includes a configurable **Target Mod ID** system that can be used to target equipment from another mod.

The repair system can identify equipment based on:

- Item tiers
- Armor materials
- Item names
- Mod namespaces

This allows Advance Repair to work with a wider variety of equipment instead of being permanently locked to vanilla items.

---

## Why Make This Mod?

Minecraft's repair system works well, but sometimes you end up with an almost-broken item and don't have the exact material required to repair it.

Maybe you have:

> A damaged diamond tool  
> No diamonds  
> Plenty of other resources

Advance Repair gives you another option.

Instead of throwing the item away or waiting until you find the exact resource, you can use an alternative material and accept a different repair efficiency.

---

## The Philosophy

Advance Repair follows three simple ideas:

### 1. More Choices

Players should have more than one way to deal with damaged equipment.

### 2. Keep It Balanced

Alternative repairs shouldn't completely replace normal resource gathering.

### 3. Keep Minecraft Feeling Like Minecraft

The mod expands the existing anvil system instead of turning it into a completely different mechanic.
